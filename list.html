How to bake the perfect Satsumaimo (Japanese sweet potato):

  1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees
  2. Wash your potato well, give it a good strub, and pat it dry.
  3. Using a fork, poke small holes all over it.
  4. Once your oven is preheated, pop your potato inside and set a timer for 1hr and 30 minutes.
  5. Optional: For extra caramelization, crank the oven to 410 for the last 10-15 minutes.
  6. *CAUTION HOT* Remove potato from oven with tongs and let it rest for 5 minutes.
  7. Slice it open and enjoy!
